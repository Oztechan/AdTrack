## What AdTrack Does

AdTrack lets you view your own Google AdMob revenue and reporting data inside the app. To do this, you sign in with your Google account and grant AdTrack **read-only** access to your AdMob data (the `admob.readonly` permission). AdTrack only requests this single permission — it does **not** receive your Google name, email address, profile, or any other Google data.

## Information Collection and Use

- **Google sign-in (OAuth):** When you sign in, Google issues AdTrack an access token and a refresh token. The **refresh token is stored only on your device**, in encrypted storage (Android `EncryptedSharedPreferences`; iOS Keychain). It is used to keep you signed in and to request fresh access tokens. It is never sent to us or to any third party other than Google.
- **AdMob data:** AdTrack reads your AdMob account information (publisher id, currency, reporting time zone) and revenue reports (estimated earnings, impressions, clicks, broken down by date, app, platform, and format) directly from Google's AdMob API. This data is fetched on demand and held **only in memory** (briefly cached while the app is open). **It is not stored on any server and we operate no backend** — the data travels directly between your device and Google.
- **No account/personal profile data** is collected by AdTrack itself (the read-only AdMob permission does not include your email, name, or user id).

## Advertising

AdTrack is ad-supported and uses Google AdMob to show ads (banner, interstitial, and rewarded). AdMob and its partners may collect and use device information and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. In regions where it is required (e.g. the EEA/UK), AdTrack shows a consent message through Google's User Messaging Platform before serving personalized ads, and on iOS it presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can use AdTrack fully regardless of your tracking choice. You may also purchase or earn an ad-free (premium) period; the premium status is stored only on your device and contains no personal data.
